HISTORY

Every loaf of bread tells a story. Since 2010, we have been baking with passion, quality, and love for our community.

ESTABLISHED IN 2010

When the bakery was established

Our bakery was established in 2010 as a small neighborhood bakery. Starting with only a few handmade bread and pastry recipes, we were committed to serving fresh, high-quality baked goods every day.

Why It Was Founded

We founded Avada Bakery our of our passion for baking and a desire to bring poeple together through delicious bread,pastries and cakes

Made With Love

Every product is baked with care and passion

Quality Ingredients

For Our Community

Meet Our Founders

Emily Lee and Daniel Lee founded the bakery in 2010. Their passion for traditional baking, creativity, and dedication to quality helped transform a small family bakery into a trusted local business.
